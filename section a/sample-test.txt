Title: Science & Engineering Mock Exam
Duration: 15

[Section: Physics (+4, -1)]

1. What is the SI unit of electric charge?
A) Volt
B) Coulomb
C) Ohm
D) Tesla
Answer: B
Explanation: The SI unit of electric charge is the Coulomb (C), where Q = I × t.

2. A ray of light enters from air into glass of refractive index 1.5. If the speed of light in vacuum is 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s, what is the speed of light in glass?
A) 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s
B) 1.5 × 10⁸ m/s
C) 2.25 × 10⁸ m/s
D) 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s
Answer: A
Explanation: Speed v = c / n = 3.0 × 10⁸ / 1.5 = 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s.

3. In an isothermal expansion of an ideal gas, which quantity remains constant?
A) Internal energy
B) Pressure
C) Volume
D) Entropy
Answer: A
Explanation: For an ideal gas, internal energy depends only on temperature (ΔU = 0).

[Section: Chemistry (+4, -1)]

4. Which quantum number specifies the spatial orientation of an atomic orbital?
A) Principal quantum number (n)
B) Azimuthal quantum number (l)
C) Magnetic quantum number (ml)
D) Spin quantum number (ms)
Answer: C
Explanation: The magnetic quantum number (ml) specifies the orbital orientation in space.

5. Which of the following molecules possesses a permanent dipole moment?
A) CO2
B) CH4
C) NH3
D) BF3
Answer: C
Explanation: NH3 has a trigonal pyramidal structure with a lone pair, making it polar.

[Section: Mathematics (+4, -1)]

6. Evaluate the limit: lim (x -> 0) [ sin(3x) / (2x) ].
A) 0
B) 1
C) 3/2
D) 2/3
Answer: C
Explanation: lim (sin(3x)/(2x)) = (3/2) * lim (sin(3x)/(3x)) = 3/2 * 1 = 3/2.

7. What is the radius of the circle: x² + y² - 6x + 8y = 0?
A) 3
B) 4
C) 5
D) 25
Answer: C
Explanation: (x - 3)² + (y + 4)² = 3² + 4² = 25. Radius r = √25 = 5.
