You are an expert Android developer and mobile systems engineer.

Build the complete codebase for **Reel Monster** (Project 6), an offline Android application that tracks short-form video consumption using a floating, draggable monster bubble that overlays Instagram and YouTube.

---

### Environmental & Platform Constraints
- **Platform**: Real Android device (API level 26+).
- **Toolchain**: Clean Gradle project compatible with JDK 17 and AGP 8.2+.
- **Zero Internet / Full Privacy**: 
  - Strictly DO NOT include `android.permission.INTERNET` in `AndroidManifest.xml`.
  - No user logins, cloud backends, tracking SDKs, or paid APIs.
  - The application must install and run entirely offline.

---

### Required Features & Architecture

#### 1. Floating Monster Bubble (`OverlayService`)
- Implemented via `WindowManager` using layout type `TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY` (requires `android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`).
- **Draggable & Snappable**:
  - Touch listener supporting smooth drag movement across the screen, snapping to the left or right edge on finger release.
  - Distinctly distinguishes short taps from drag actions.
- **Visuals & Dynamic Expressions**:
  - A circular cute monster avatar that dynamically scales up / changes facial expressions as the daily counter rises:
    - 0–5 reels: Normal cute smile.
    - 6–15 reels: Smirking, slightly larger.
    - 16–30 reels: Bloated / chubby with tired eyes.
    - 31+ reels: Massive overwhelmed monster with sweat drops.
  - Prominent badge on the avatar showing the live reel count.
  - Floating comic speech bubble displaying humorous, sarcastic one-liners triggered at milestones (e.g., at 5 reels: *"Yum! Your evening tastes great."*; at 15: *"Burp. Your homework says hi."*; at 30: *"Still scrolling? Your future self is calling."*).

#### 2. Dual Counting Modes (Configurable in Dashboard)
- **Level 1 (Manual Mode)**:
  - Tapping the floating monster bubble increments the counter by +1, displays a quick bounce animation, and surfaces a funny dialogue bubble.
- **Level 2 (Automatic Detection Mode)**:
  - Implements an Android `AccessibilityService` (`ReelAccessibilityService`).
  - Listens to scroll events (`TYPE_VIEW_SCROLLED`, `TYPE_WINDOW_CONTENT_CHANGED`) strictly targeted at:
    - Instagram: `com.instagram.android`
    - YouTube Shorts: `com.google.android.youtube`
  - Uses debouncing logic (minimum 1.5–2.0 second cooldown) to prevent duplicate counts during rapid layout shifts or fast scrolling.
  - Accuracy requirement: Across a continuous test of 20 reels, the recorded count must match within ±2 of manual observation.
- **Overlay Visibility**:
  - Dynamically displays the floating bubble when Instagram or YouTube is open in the foreground, with an option to minimize or stay visible across other apps.

#### 3. Persistence & Reboot Resilience (`BootReceiver` & Local Storage)
- Stores daily counts and history locally using `SharedPreferences` or an offline SQLite database keyed by date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- Implements a `BroadcastReceiver` listening to `android.intent.action.BOOT_COMPLETED` (with permission `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) to ensure today's count survives a device restart and automatically restores the overlay if enabled.

#### 4. Dashboard Activity (`MainActivity`)
- Clean, offline Material Design dashboard showing:
  - Today's reel count and calculated time lost (estimated at 45 seconds per reel, e.g., `12 reels • 9m lost`).
  - Rolling 7-day bar chart / list summary of reels watched.
  - Mode toggle: **Level 1 (Manual Tap)** vs. **Level 2 (Automatic Detection)**.
  - Permission setup guides and intent launchers:
    - "Display over other apps" (`Settings.ACTION_MANAGE_OVERLAY_PERMISSION`).
    - "Accessibility Service" (`Settings.ACTION_ACCESSIBILITY_SETTINGS`) for Level 2 mode.
  - Reset / Test button to zero out counts for verification runs.
